Our Story

Why We Started GLM

GLM Landscape is a full-service landscaping and outdoor living company serving Las Cruces, New Mexico and the surrounding areas. We specialize in creating beautiful, low‑maintenance outdoor spaces through custom landscape design, artificial turf, hardscaping, rock installation, planting, and outdoor lighting.

We started GLM to build a company that homeowners can trust — with honest communication, quality workmanship, and a better customer experience, creating outdoor spaces that add value and can be enjoyed for years to come.
